foldable. practical. robust.

Covering distance. Travelling light. Classic folding design. 100 % UV protection and uvex supravision® anti-fog coating combine to make the uvex mtn tour the ideal choice for your peak performance.

Lightweight. Compact. Stowable. Using the latest technology, the uvex mtn tour aren’t just a pair of performance sports glasses but a functional masterpiece. The uvex mtn tour sunglasses are foldable thanks to a flexible section at the nose bridge. Which means they fit neatly into a jacket chest pocket. Designed to excel equally at ascents and descents, these glasses are perfect for mountaineers who like to keep things fast and light. And the optical quality is outstanding too. They filter out 100 % of all UVA, UVB and UVC rays. uvex supravision® anti-fog lens coating guarantees zero lens misting for clear vision in the most challenging conditions. Interchangeable lens technology lets you swap to clear lenses for poor conditions. Stylishly packed away, and safe on the move. The uvex mtn tour – a mountaineering legend in the making.

Technologies included

mist free. reliable. safer.

Mist-free vision. Clear optics. Reliable clarity – which means better safety in poor conditions. That’s thanks to uvex supravision® lens technology. A special anti-fog coating prevents lenses from misting up, without affecting optical quality. And for twice as long as the standard. Pure optical joy.
