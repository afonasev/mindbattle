  • Electric Start or Rope Pull

    Electric start option is available for both gas and propane models. (Propane option may not be available in all markets.)

  • Adjustable Board Guide

    Contoured snow chute directs snow toward center of ice, away from the boards and operator. Adjustable board guide is standard.

  • Wide 18" Cutting Path with Long-Lasting 8-Sided Cutters

    Wide 18-inch (457mm) cut from eight-sided cutters that last longer, saving replacement time and cost. Depth adjustment is close at hand for ease of operation.
