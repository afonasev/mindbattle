Tbe Friday mosque
1172 is the date when caliph Abu Yaqub or-
dered his architect, Ahmad b. Baso, to con-
struct this huge 8,231 square metre building. 
The first liturgy (jutba) to take place there was 
on April 30th, 1182. After Abu Yaqub's death 
in 1184, his successor continued the project 
from 1188 onwards with a new architect 
called Ali Gomair. In this period, the courtyard 
(sahn) and the minaret were completed.
Today the sahn and the minaret are still partially 
preserved; the haram (oratory) is under the Gothic 
cathedral. The orientation of the mosque, follow-
ing the convention in al-Andalus, was south-north. 
From 1984 onwards, several archaeological 
excavations have been carried out in the ora-
tory and the courtyard, in addition to the re-
storation of the minaret. Also, in 1994, a wash- 
room was discovered opposite the minaret. 
This research, which is to be published forth-
with, will provide the key to a definitive un-
derstanding of the building and its signifi-
cance.
