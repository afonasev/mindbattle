Reacts in seconds for the best vision – all the time

Looking for an all-mountain visor helmet that gives you top-flight vision in any weather? We’ve got it. In a super-cool design.

You know what it’s like. Just now you could gaze out over snow-covered peaks and slopes and suddenly the light or weather changes. Your visor should notice it too! The uvex wanted visor pro V does exactly that – with its self-tinting variomatic® visor and reaction in seconds. The result: the best vision in any weather. Plus: the integrated, active ventilation system ensures a comfortable helmet temperature. No question: this helmet is our robust all-rounder that protects you in every situation. You’ll also love its cool look and clean design.

Product-Highlights

  • Clear vision: uvex variomatic® self-tinting lens technology for clear vision in all conditions
  • Reliable protection: extremely impact resistant ABS hardshell and shock-absorbing EPS inner shell
  • Easy handling: magnetic Fidlock® buckle
  • Precise custom fit: uvex IAS system
  • Temperature regulation: adjustable ventilation system throughout

Technologies included

smooth. adaptive. safer.

Smooth transition. Adaptive tint. Flexible eye protection – no matter what the light. That’s thanks to uvex variomatic® photochromic lenses. They automatically adjust light transmission and smoothly switch through categories 0-4. No lens changing required.

strong. secure. intuitive.

Secure fit. Better safety. Easy to use. Lock it in! The FIDLOCK magnetic buckle uses powerful magnets for one-handed operation, even when wearing gloves. The audible click sound signals its secure fastening. Safety first!
