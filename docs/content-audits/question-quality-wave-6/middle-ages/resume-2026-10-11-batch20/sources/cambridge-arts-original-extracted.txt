L67: Arts
L68: From the earliest times all students for higher degrees had first to cite47†graduate in the junior cite48†faculty of arts. In medieval times the curriculum in arts consisted of the cite49†trivium (grammar, rhetoric and logic), for the cite50†BA , followed by the cite51†quadrivium (arithmetic, music, geometry and astronomy) which, with the three philosophies (moral, natural and metaphysical), formed the basis of the cite52†MA course.
L69: The term is now commonly used, often in the phrase ‘arts and humanities’ in distinction from the natural and social sciences.
