L222: # Things To See and Do
L223: 
L224: There’s lots to see and do at Wenlock Priory. Below, you’ll find details about the chapter house, the cloister garden and the medieval floor tiles to look out for on your visit.
L225: ## Explore the Chapter House
L226: 
L227: Discover the Norman chapter house at Wenlock Priory, built around 1140 as the 'business' centre of the monastery. It's here that the monks and the prior met each morning to discuss affairs, and administer punishments for disobedience.
L228: 
L229: Today, you can still see much of the chapter house's elaborate stone carving, with interlocking round arches on multiple carved columns.
L230: Don't miss the grotesque head, humorously carved in the lintel of the doorway.
L231: ## Unique Cloister Garden
L232: 
L233: Take time to enjoy the peace and tranquillity of the topiary-filled cloister garden.
L234: This cloister garden is set against the backdrop of the complete infirmary wing, converted into a mansion after the priory's dissolution. It's still a private residence today.
L235: 
L236: Look out for the unusual octagonal lavabo, the huge water vessel built around 1220 and used by monks to wash their hands before eating in the nearby refectory.
L237: Embellished with 12th-century carvings, depicting Christ and the apostles, free-standing lavabos of this kind are rarely seen in the United Kingdom.
L238: ## Admire the Medieval Tiles
L239: 
L240: Visit the priory's library and discover the locally-made medieval floor tiles.
L241: 
L242: These tiles have been carefully re-laid to give an impression of how they would have originally appeared.
L243: 
L244: Notice the difference in the three doorways of the library. The central archway was the original entrance; the other two were added after the Dissolution of the Monasteries, when the priory was used as a farm.
L245: ## Build your own … city
L246: 
L247: Imagine you’re standing in an English city hundreds of years ago. What’s around you? A bustling market, bells ringing, a new church being built, the smell of animals...
L248: 
L249: This is the starting point of our city trail (one of four World Builder Challenge trails at our sites). Follow the markers and build your very own city. Be inspired by the past and let your imagination run wild as you create a whole new city for your people to live in.
