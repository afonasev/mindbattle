L489@P15: H. Deck
L490@P15: ◆ When instructed to draw cards, draw the specified number of cards from the top of your deck,
L491@P15: face down, and put them into your hand.
L492@P15: ◆ When instructed to search your deck for cards, look at the contents of your deck and choose the
L493@P15: specified number of cards. While searching through your deck, its contents should be visible to
L494@P15: you but not to your opponent. When searching for several cards, you may decide to pick less than
L495@P15: the number specified. You may also decide to not pick any cards at all, in which case the search
L496@P15: ends. However, if instructed to search for any card(s) you want without any limitations on the
L497@P15: type of card, you must pick the specified number of cards, no less.
L498@P15: ◆ When instructed to shuffle your deck, the order of the contents of your deck should be properly
L499@P15: randomized, so that neither player has any information on the order of the cards. When shuffling
L500@P15: your deck, neither player should see the face of the cards. As a general rule, whenever an effect
L501@P15: such as “search your deck for ●● and put it into your hand” lets you look at the contents of your
L502@P15-16: deck, you must then shuffle your deck.Pokémon Card Game Advanced Player’s Rulebook Ver. 3.4 17

L507@P16: A. About Card Text
L508@P16: ◆ Whenever a card’s text contradicts the basic rules, the card’s text takes priority over the basic
L509@P16: rules. However, if a card’s text has been updated at any point, the latest version of the effect
L510@P16: must be applied.
L511@P16: ◆ If two effects stating “do ●●” and “can’t ●●” are active at the same time, the “can’t ●●” effect
L512@P16: takes priority.
L513@P16: ◆ Attacks, Abilities or Trainers can be used even if part of their instructions cannot be applied. In
L514@P16: such cases, follow the parts of the instructions that you can apply, and ignore the parts you
L515@P16: can’t. However, some wordings such as E-20: Do ●●. If you do, do ■■ make it so that some parts
L516@P16: of the instructions are mandatory in order to apply other parts.
L517@P16: ◆ If a card’s text specifies a number, you cannot choose to make it a smaller number. There are
L518@P16: some exceptions: please refer to D-04: Choose and D-05: Up to ■■ ●●.
L519@P16: ◆ If a card’s text specifies a number of something, but there are not enough of that thing
L520@P16: available, apply the closest possible number instead.
