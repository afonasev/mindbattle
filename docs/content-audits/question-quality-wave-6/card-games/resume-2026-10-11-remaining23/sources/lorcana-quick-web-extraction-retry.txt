L8@P0-1: QUICK START RULESWhat Kind of Game is This?
L9@P1: The Disney Lorcana Trading Card Game is a
L10@P1: strategy card game where each player makes
L11@P1: their own deck before the game starts, and the
L12@P1: decks aren’t mixed together. You can play with
L13@P1: one of the available ready-to-play decks, make
L14@P1: changes to one of those decks, or build a deck
L15@P1: that’s entirely your own!
L16@P1: In a game like this, cards can allow you to do
L17@P1: things that aren’t normally a part of every
L18@P1: turn. If a card’s text ever contradicts the game
L19@P1: rules, follow what the card says.
L20@P1: Winning
L21@P1: Your goal is to be the first player to gain 20 or more lore.
L22@P1: Some card abilities give you lore, but the most common
L23@P1: way to gain it is by playing characters and sending them on
L24@P1: quests.

L29@P1: at a game store near you and download the
L30@P1: Disney Lorcana Trading Card Game Companion

L272@P9: Some locations have abilities that give you benefits, often
L273@P9: based on characters being at the location. These abilities can
L274@P9: be used the same turn the location comes into play.
L275@P9: Moving Characters to Locations
L276@P9: During your turn, you can move one of your characters to
L277@P9: one of your locations by paying the location’s move cost .
L278@P9: Your characters can move only to your locations.
L279@P9: A character can move to a location the same turn either one
L280@P9: is played. It doesn’t matter if the character being moved is
L281@P9: exerted or ready. There’s no limit to the number of characters
L282@P9: who can be at a location at a time.
L283@P9: A character can’t move from a location except to move
L284@P9: to another location. You can do this by paying the other
L285@P9: location’s move cost.
L286@P9: Location Challenges & Damage
L287@P9: On their turn, opposing characters may challenge a location
L288@P9: the same way they’d challenge an exerted character. A
L289@P9: character’s Challenger ability applies when challenging
L290@P9: locations. When a location is challenged, it takes damage
L291@P9: equal to the challenging character’s Strength {S}. It doesn’t
L292@P9: deal damage of its own in the challenge, however.
L293@P9: Locations can also take damage from some actions and
L294@P9: abilities. Locations can’t be chosen for actions and abilities
L295@P9: unless that text specifies a location can be chosen.
L296@P9: When a location has damage equal to or greater than its
L297@P9: Willpower {W}, it’s banished and you put that card in your
L298@P9: discard pile.
L299@P9: Characters who are at a location when it’s banished simply
L300@P9: find themselves abroad again. They’re fine; they’re just no
L301@P9: longer at a location.

L311@P10: MULTIPLAYER GAMES
L312@P10: This is a fun 2-player game, but there’s nothing stopping you
L313@P10: from playing games of 3 or more players!
L314@P10: The game rules remain the same except that at the end of a
L315@P10: player’s turn, the player to their left takes the next turn.
L316@P10: Whenever an ability requires more than one player to do
L317@P10: something at the same time, start with the player whose
L318@P10: turn it is, then proceed to the left until each affected player
L319@P10: has done their action.
L320@P10: Using Abilities
L321@P10: Many items and characters have abilities you can use during
L322@P10: your turn (and only during your turn). These normally affect
L323@P10: other cards in play. Cards in a player’s deck, discard, hand,
L324@P10: or inkwell aren’t “in play,” so they aren’t affected by other
L325@P10: cards unless the card says otherwise.
L326@P10: A card ability that has a cost lists it before the effect. The
L327@P10: cost might include an exert cost {E}, an ink cost {I}, text that
L328@P10: explains the cost, or a combination of any of these. You must
L329@P10: pay every part of an ability’s cost in order to play the ability.
L330@P10: Remember, you can’t use the abilities of a character you
L331@P10: played this turn.
L332@P10: Example: Merlin’s Carpetbag has the ability Hockety
L333@P10: Pockety, which reads, “{E}, 1 {I} — Return an item card from
L334@P10: your discard to your hand.” On your turn, you may use this
L335@P10: ability by exerting the card and paying 1 {I} (by exerting 1
L336@P10: card in your inkwell.) Because the Carpetbag is an item, you
L337@P10: can even use its ability the same turn you played the card!

L339@P11: BUILDING A DECK
L340@P11: Making your own deck is part of the fun! You get to choose
L341@P11: which characters you want, which abilities you include, and
L342@P11: the strategies you want to employ.
L343@P11: There are 2 ways to approach building a deck. The easiest is
L344@P11: to switch out cards in an existing deck, like one of the readyto-play starter decks. You can also build a new deck from
L345@P11: scratch using the cards in your collection. This method is
L346@P11: more work, but it can be a lot of fun!
L347@P11: Each Disney Lorcana TCG deck must follow these rules:
L348@P11: ♦ Your deck must have at least 60 cards in it.
