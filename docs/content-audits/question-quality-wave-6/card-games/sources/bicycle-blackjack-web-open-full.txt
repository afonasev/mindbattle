L9 # Blackjack

L25 Blackjack is a fast, social casino classic where players compete only against the dealer, not each other. The goal is simple: build a hand closer to 21 than the dealer without busting. With quick decisions, flexible Aces, and a mix of luck and strategy, it’s one of the easiest card games to learn - and one of the most replayable.
L26 The Pack
L28 52 cards, no jokers
L30 In casinos, multiple decks are often shuffled together and dealt from a dealing shoe.
L32 Object of the Game
L34 To beat the dealer, getting as close to 21 without going over.
L36 Card Values/scoring
L38 Face Cards = 10
L40 Ace = 1 or 11
L42 Number Cards = Pip Value
L44 Setup
L46 Each player places an initial bet before cards are dealt.
L48 Players receive two cards face-up.
L50 The dealer receives one card face-up and one face-down (the “hole” card).
L51 Players Turn
L53 Evaluate your hand
L55 Add your two card values together
L57 Choose your action
L59 Hit: Take another card to add to your total
L61 Stand/Stay: Keep your current total. End your turn.
L63 Double Down: Double your bet, take one final card, then stand.
L65 Split: If you have a pair, split into two hands by placing a second bet equal to your first.
L66 Dealer’s Turn
L68 Dealer reveals the hole card.
L70 Dealer must hit until reaching 17 or higher.
L72 If the dealer busts (goes over 21), all remaining players win.
L74 Payouts
L76 Win: Paid 1:1.
L78 Blackjack (Ace + 10-value card): Paid 3:2.
L80 Push (tie): Your bet is returned.
L82 Lose: Dealer collects your bet.
L83 Insurance
L85 Insurance is an optional side bet offered only when the dealer’s face-up card is an Ace.
L87 You may place an insurance bet equal to half your original wager.
L89 This bet pays 2:1 only if the dealer’s hidden card is a 10-value card, giving them blackjack.
L91 The outcome of your insurance bet is separate from the outcome of your main hand.
L92 Strategy Tips
L94 Stand on 12-16 when the dealer shows 2-6. The dealer is more likely to bust.
L96 Hit 12-16 when the dealer shows 7-Ace. You need to improve your hand.
L98 Always split Aces and 8s. Aces give you two strong starts; 8s get you out of a weak 16.
L100 Never split 10s. A 20 is already a winning hand.
L102 Double down on 10 or 11. These give you the best chance to win with one card.
L104 Avoid insurance. Over time, the bet loses more than it wins.
