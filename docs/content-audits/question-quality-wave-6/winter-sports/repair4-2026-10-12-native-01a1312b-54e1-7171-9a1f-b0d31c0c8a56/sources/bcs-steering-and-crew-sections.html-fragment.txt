<h2>Competition Equipment</h2>

<p>The bobsleigh is very aerodynamic, with the cowling made of fiberglass, which is constructed onto a steel chassis/frame. The sled &quot;runs&quot; on four highly polished steel runners. The two front-runners are mounted on a steering pin, which have approximately three inches of lateral movement and are controlled by ropes held by the driver who steers the sled. The brake handles are located on either side of the brakeman in the four-man sled and in front of the brakeman in the two-man sled.</p>

<h2>Sport Description</h2>

<p>Bobsleigh teams consist of a brakeman and a pilot in the two-man event and a brakeman, two crewmen and a pilot in the four-man.</p>
