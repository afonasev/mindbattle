Stylishly understated. Technically refined. Powerful looks: the uvex victorious FM. With wide field of vision, clear optics and the highest UV protection. Top performance in a mirrored package.

Stylishly understated. Technically refined. Powerful looks. Thanks to the uvex victorious FM with wide field of vision, clear optics and the highest UV protection. With a mirrored lens, the uvex victorious FM goggles stand out from the crowd. But it’s not just about the looks – the spherical mirrored double lens also works well in bright sunshine and changeable conditions on the piste. It blocks out 100% of UVA, UVB and UVC rays up to 400 nm, which is above the norm standard. The uvex supravision® anti-fog coating guarantees mist-free lenses. And a 50-mm-wide head strap with silicone grippers adds an extra dash of style. Plus, the Over the Glasses (OTG) design makes them a hot tip for people who wear glasses. Top performance at a reasonable price. What’s not to like?

Product-Highlights

  • Sun protection: 100 % UVA, UVB, UVC protection up to 400 nm
  • Proven technology: spherical mirrored double lens
  • Mist-free lens: uvex supravision® anti-fog coating
  • Snug fit: flexible frame with double-layer velour face foam and a 50-mm-wide head strap with silicone grippers for a secure fit
  • Glasses compatible: Over the Glasses (OTG) design

Technologies included

mist free. reliable. safer.

Mist-free vision. Clear optics. Reliable clarity – which means better safety in poor conditions. That’s thanks to uvex supravision® lens technology. A special anti-fog coating prevents lenses from misting up, without affecting optical quality. And for twice as long as the standard. Pure optical joy.
