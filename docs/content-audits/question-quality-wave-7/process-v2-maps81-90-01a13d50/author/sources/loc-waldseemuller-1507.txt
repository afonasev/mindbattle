L15-L16: # Exploring the Early Americas; Waldseemüller Maps
L19: Martin Waldseemüller's 1507 world map grew out of a project to document new geographic knowledge from late-fifteenth- and early-sixteenth-century discoveries, including information from Amerigo Vespucci's 1501–1502 voyages.
L20: Waldseemüller named the new lands “America” in recognition of Vespucci's understanding that a new continent had been uncovered. This is the only known surviving copy of the 1,000 maps believed printed.
L21: The map was the first to depict clearly a separate Western Hemisphere apart from Asia, with the Pacific as a separate ocean.
