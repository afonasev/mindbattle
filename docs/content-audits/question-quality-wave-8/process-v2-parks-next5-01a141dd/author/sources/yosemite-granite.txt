# Granite
Granite dominates the geology of Yosemite National Park. The park boundary frames a landscape composed almost entirely of granite and containing some of the most iconic rock formations on earth. The southeast face of El Capitan includes the older and lighter-colored El Capitan and Taft granites, with darker Diorite of North America. Plutons are named after famous landmarks dominantly made of their rock; examples include Half Dome Granodiorite and El Capitan Granite. Yosemite is known throughout the world for its exceptional high cliffs and rounded domes.
