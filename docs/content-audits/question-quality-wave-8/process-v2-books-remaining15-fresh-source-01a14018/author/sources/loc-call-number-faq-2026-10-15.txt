Library of Congress — What’s a call number and how do I use it?
Full official FAQ body read via official page rendering on 2026-10-15.

Call numbers are numbers on book spines/covers and in catalog records. They are used to find and organize materials on shelves. Letters and numbers provide a specific shelf location; labels are usually on spine or front cover. Subject areas are arranged alphabetically and numerically.
