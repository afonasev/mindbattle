Publications Division, Ministry of Information & Broadcasting, India — Royalty Structure and Copyright
Complete relevant official government page read in browser rendering on 2026-10-15.

Royalty payment is 15% of print price under the described contract. Authors may retain copyright or transfer it to Publications Division. With retained copyright, an advance is calculated for 50% of the first print order, adjusted against book sales, and further royalty at 15% is paid after the advance is settled.
