PRINTING United Alliance — Printing industry and Books Printing definitions

Relevant complete context (web-reader PDF pp. 0–1, 3–4; lines 30–40, 89–115, 152–160): books printing establishments primarily engage in “printing or printing and binding books and pamphlets without publishing”; support activities include prepress/postpress, trade binding, folding and gluing. Printing establishments may print and perform postprinting activities. This is an industry association submission to the U.S. Office of Management and Budget.
