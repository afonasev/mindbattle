L348: # Chapter 3.6: Thrust Bearings
L349: 
L350: Thrust bearings use balls or rollers to support axial loads (loads in the direction of the rotating shaft).
L351: #### Thrust Ball Bearings
L352:   * The raceways of thrust ball bearings lie on housing washers and shaft washers, which serve the same function as the outer and inner ring in radial bearings.
L353:   * Double-direction types feature a central washer attached to the shaft.
L354:   * Single-direction types can support axial loads in one direction, while double- direction types support axial loads in both directions.
L355:   * Some models have an aligning seat attached to the housing washer to reduce the effect of mounting errors.
L356:   * Cages are typically made of pressed steel for small bearings, while large bearings use machined cages.
L357: cite180†Image: Types and Parts of Thrust Ball Bearings L358: #### Spherical Roller Thrust Bearings
L359:   * The barrel-shaped rollers in spherical roller thrust bearings are arranged to create diagonal rolling surfaces. Since the housing raceway is spherical, the bearing is self-aligning.
L360:   * Spherical roller thrust bearings have very high capacity for axial (thrust) loads and can carry some radial load when an axial load is applied.
L361:   * Oil lubrication is recommended even at low speeds to maintain proper lubrication in areas such as the contact surfaces between the roller and shaft washer rib.
L362:   * The raceways of thrust roller bearings lie on housing washers and shaft washers, which serve the same function as the outer and inner ring in radial bearings.
