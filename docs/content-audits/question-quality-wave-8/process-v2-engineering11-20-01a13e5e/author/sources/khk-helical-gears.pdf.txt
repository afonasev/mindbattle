L236@P2: ① If reboring, it is important to pay special attention to
L237@P2: locating the center in order to avoid runout.

L239@P2: Therefore, use the bore for locating the center. If it is too
L240@P2: difficult to do for small bores, the alternative is to use
L241@P2: one spot on the bore and the runout of the side surface.
L242@P2: ③ If reworking using scroll chucks, we recommend the use
L243@P2: of new or rebored jaws for improved precision. Please
L244@P2: exercise caution not to crush the teeth.
L245@P2: ④ The maximum bore size is dictated by the requirement
L246@P2: that the strength of the hub is to be higher than that of
L247@P2: the gear teeth. The maximum bore size should be 60%
L248@P2: to 70% of the hub diameter (or tooth root diameter), and
L249@P2: 50% to 60% for keyway applied modifications.
L250@P2: ⑤ In order to avoid stress concentration, round the keyway
L251@P2: corners.
L252@P2: ⑥ To avoid problems of reduced gear precision and other
L253@P2: manufacturing difficulties, do not attempt to machine
L254@P2: the gears to reduce face widths.
L255@P2: ⑦ When induction-hardening S45C products, thermal
L256@P2: stress cracks may appear. Also, note that the precision
L257@P2: grade of the product declines by 1 or 2 grades, as
L258@P2: deformation on material may occur. If you require
L259@P2: tolerance for bore or other parts, machining is necessary
L260@P2: after heat treatment.
