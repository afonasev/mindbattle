L36: ### Leave No Trace
L37: Imagine you are hiking a trail with a few friends. Your group notices some trash and litter along the side of the towpath. How do you think waste impacts your environment? Does it make your trip even a little less enjoyable? Practicing the 7 Leave No Trace (LNT) principles is an important duty for all Canal Stewards. The LNT principles guide you to recreate sustainably in your National Parks. Review the 7 principles below then test your skills by playing cite4†Leave No Trace Charades with friends!
L38: #### cite5†Principle #1 – Plan Ahead and Prepare L39: 
L40:   * Know the regulations and special concerns for the area you'll visit.
L41:   * Prepare for extreme weather, hazards, and emergencies.
L42:   * Schedule your trip to avoid times of high use.
L43:   * Visit in small groups when possible. Consider splitting larger groups into smaller groups.
L44:   * Repackage food to minimize waste.
L45:   * Use a map and compass or GPS to eliminate the use of marking paint, rock cairns, or flagging.
L46: #### cite6†Principle #2 – Travel and Camp on Durable Surfaces L47:   * Durable surfaces include maintained trails and designated campsites, rock, gravel, sand, dry grasses or snow.
L48:   * Protect riparian areas by camping at least 200 feet from lakes and streams.
L49:   * Good campsites are found, not made. Altering a site is not necessary.
L50:     * In popular areas:
L51:       * Concentrate use on existing trails and campsites.
L52:       * Walk single file in the middle of the trail, even when wet or muddy.
L53:       * Keep campsites small. Focus activity in areas where vegetation is absent.
L54:     * In pristine areas:
L55:       * Disperse use to prevent the creation of campsites and trails.
L56:       * Avoid places where impacts are just beginning.
L57: #### cite7†Principle #3 – Dispose of Waste Properly L58:   * Pack it in, pack it out. Inspect your campsite, food preparation areas, and rest areas for trash or spilled foods. Pack out all trash, leftover food and litter.
L59:   * Utilize toilet facilities whenever possible. Otherwise, deposit solid human waste in catholes dug 6 to 8 inches deep, at least 200 feet from water, camp and trails. Cover and disguise the cathole when finished.
L60:   * Pack out toilet paper and hygiene products.
L61:   * To wash yourself or your dishes, carry water 200 feet away from streams or lakes and use small amounts of biodegradable soap. Scatter strained dishwater.
L62: #### cite8†Principle #4 – Leave What You Find L63: 
L64:   * Preserve the past: examine, photograph, but do not touch cultural or historic structures and artifacts.
L65:   * Leave rocks, plants and other natural objects as you find them.
L66:   * Avoid introducing or transporting non-native species.
L67:   * Do not build structures, furniture, or dig trenches.
L68: #### cite9†Principle #5 – Minimize Campfire Impacts L69: 
L70:   * Campfires can cause lasting impacts to the environment. Use a lightweight stove for cooking and enjoy a candle lantern for light.
L71:   * Where fires are permitted, use established fire rings, fire pans, or mound fires.
L72:   * Keep fires small. Only use down and dead wood from the ground that can be broken by hand.
L73:   * Burn all wood and coals to ash, put out campfires completely, then scatter cool ashes.
L74: #### cite10†Principles #6 – Respect Wildlife L75: 
L76:   * Observe wildlife from a distance. Do not follow or approach them.
L77:   * Never feed animals. Feeding wildlife damages their health, alters natural behaviors, [habituates them to humans], and exposes them to predators and other dangers.
L78:   * Protect wildlife and your food by storing rations and trash securely.
L79:   * Control pets at all times, or leave them at home.
L80:   * Avoid wildlife during sensitive times: mating, nesting, raising young, or winter.
L81: #### cite11†Principles #7 – Be Considerate of Others L82: 
L83:   * Respect other visitors and protect the quality of their experience.
L84:   * Be courteous. Yield to other users on the trail.
L85:   * Step to the downhill side of the trail when encountering pack stock.
L86:   * Take breaks and camp away from trails and other visitors.
L87:   * Let nature's sounds prevail. Avoid loud voices and noises.
