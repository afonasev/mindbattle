L131: Measure the angles of each component in your drivetrain—driving member, driveshaft(s), driven member—to find your u-joint operating angles.
L132: A universal joint operating angle is the angle that occurs between the driving member and driveshaft, and between the driven member and driveshaft when they are not vertically aligned.
L133: 
L134: NOTE: This calculator does not address compound drive angles (horizontal offsets).
L135: 
L136: You’ll need a spirit level or digital protractor accurate to ¼ degree. You’ll be measuring angles at various points:
L137: 
L138:   * From the transmission
L139:   * Along the drivelines
L140:   * Back to the rear axles
L141: Enter the angle to the nearest ¼ degree, along with the slope (up or down):
L142: 
L143:   * Up: Rises from front to rear of vehicle
L144:   * Down: Descends from front to rear
L145: 
L146: Three ways to measure:
L147: 
L148:   * Use an adapter for a digital protractor that allows for measurement of slopes directly off bearing caps in light duty or quick disconnect applications
L149:   * Remove bearing cap and take measurement directly off yoke
L150:   * Remove snap ring and take measurement off bearing cap using adapter
L151: 
L152: Measurement tips:
L153:   * Measure along actual center line of output shaft
L154:   * Measure on flat surface 90 degrees to or parallel to output shaft of transmission
L155:   * Measure true center line
L156: 
L157: Basic rules:
L158:   * Universal joint operating angles at each end of a driveshaft should always be at least one-half degree
L159:   * Universal joint operating angles on each end of a driveshaft should always be equal within one degree of each other (one half degree for motor homes and shafts in front of transfer cases or auxiliary devices)
L160:   * For vibration-free performance, universal joint operating angles should not be larger than three degrees. If they are, make sure they do not exceed the maximum recommended angles.
L161: Driveshaft RPM  | Max. Operating Angle  | Interaxle
L162: --- | --- | ---

L171: 1500  | 11.5°  | —  | —
L172: Points to remember:
L173: 
L174:   * The angles shown on the above chart are the maximum u-joint operating angles recommended by Spicer engineers and are directly related to the speed of the driveshaft.
L175:   * Any universal joint operating angle greater than 3 degrees will lower universal joint life and may cause a vibration.
L176:   * Remember to check maximum safe driveshaft RPM by using the cite49†Critical Speed RPM Calculator .
