Relevant source text (line locators refer to the web-reader rendering):
L2-L6: Polar bears evolved to use the Arctic sea-ice niche and are distributed across most ice-covered seas of the Northern Hemisphere, including the United States, Canada, Greenland, Norway and Russia; they are generally limited to areas where sea ice covers much of the year.
L14-L18: Across most of their range, bears remain on sea ice year-round or spend only short periods on land; the source lists Arctic seas and the Canadian Arctic archipelago.
L199-L204: FWS states that polar bears use sea ice as a platform to hunt and feed on seals, seek mates and breed, travel to maternity denning areas, and move long distances.
