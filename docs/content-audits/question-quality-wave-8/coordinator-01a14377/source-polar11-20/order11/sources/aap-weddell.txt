L297-L307: Weddell seal; circumpolar coastal distribution around fast ice, with short foraging excursions into the Southern Ocean.
L307-L308: haul out on fast ice to rest and moult; females pup there.
L315-L321: breathe air through openings in the ice; stable fast ice is used for haul-out and pupping.
