OCLC — Introduction to the Dewey Decimal Classification
URL: https://www.oclc.org/content/dam/oclc/webdewey/help/introduction1.pdf
Read: PDF pp. xxx–xxxii, §§4.1–4.16; opened and personally read 2026-10-16

The official introduction says the DDC uses Arabic numerals; at top level it has ten main classes, each divided into ten divisions and sections. Its summary lists 800 Literature and 900 History & geography. Section 4.15 says the first digit in a three-digit number is the main class, the second the division, and the third the section; the notation uses at least three digits. Short exact source phrases retained: “800 Literature”; “900 History & geography”; “Arabic numerals”. The document also explains that the decimal point follows the third digit and is a visual pause, not a mathematical decimal point.
