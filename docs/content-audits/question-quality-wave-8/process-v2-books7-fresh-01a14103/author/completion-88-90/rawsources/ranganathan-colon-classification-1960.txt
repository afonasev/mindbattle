Primary author text: the title/edition pages identify Ranganathan and the 1960 sixth edition. In §§002–0031, Ranganathan contrasts Colon Classification with decimal and Congress schedules, says ready-made numbers are not assigned to subjects, and explains construction by combining standard unit-schedule numbers in prescribed permutations. He explicitly identifies the colon and other connecting symbols as the joins in that construction. §§006 and 008 explain decimal-fractional notation and that Chapter 05 treats facets and connecting symbols.
