LOC training says there are 21 main LCC classes and over 225 subclasses. It describes LCC notation as alphanumeric: each classification number begins with one or more letters followed by a number, optionally decimal; additional letters and numbers follow a defined order. This is used as an own-option fact in the LCC note. Short exact phrase retained: “LCC uses alphanumeric notation.”
