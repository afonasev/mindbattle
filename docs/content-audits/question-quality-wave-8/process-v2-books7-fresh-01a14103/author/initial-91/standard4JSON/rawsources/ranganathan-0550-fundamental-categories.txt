S. R. Ranganathan, Colon Classification: Basic Classification, sixth edition 1960, title/edition lines 302–331. Rules §0550, lines 2850–2859, says every facet and focus is treated as a manifestation of one of five fundamental categories. §05501, lines 2857–2879, tables fundamental categories, connecting symbols and facet symbols: Personality—comma—[P]; Matter—semicolon—[M]; Energy—colon—[E]; Space—dot—[S]; Time—dot—[T] in this edition. OCR misspells Personality in one table cell; the contractions at lines 3608–3624 spell it and all facet symbols clearly. §05502, lines 2884–2889, says isolate numbers are preceded by the prescribed connecting symbol unless a facet formula or rule indicates omission. §085, lines 3814–3822, separately gives PME ordering within a round and places S/T in the last round. This packet relies only on the unambiguous P/M/E mappings, not on different signs for S and T.
