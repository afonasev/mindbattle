bookmark, noun: 1) (also bookmarker) a marker for finding a place in a book; 2) a menu entry or icon on a computer, usually created by the user, serving as a shortcut to a previously viewed location (such as an Internet site). Verb: to create a computer bookmark for.

First known noun use in sense 1: 1833; first known verb use: 1985. Citation block: “Bookmark.” Merriam-Webster.com Dictionary, Merriam-Webster, https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/bookmark. Page last updated 10 October 2026 (examples updated).
