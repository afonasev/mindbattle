Purdue OWL says to include a page header (also known as the “running head”) at the top of every page. A professional paper's header includes its title and page number; a student paper's includes only the page number. The running head is a shortened version of the paper title, with a limit of 50 characters including spacing and punctuation. The page notes that page headers/page numbers appear at the top of the title page too.

This concerns APA papers; paired with the Merriam-Webster book definition, it supports upper placement and the running-head term without conflating it with a lower-page footer.
