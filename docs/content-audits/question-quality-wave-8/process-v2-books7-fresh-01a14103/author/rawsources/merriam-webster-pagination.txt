pagination, noun: 1) the action of paging; the condition of being paged; 2a) the numbers or marks used to indicate the sequence of pages (as of a book); 2b) the number and arrangement of pages or an indication of these.

The first known use for sense 2a is 1794. Citation block: “Pagination.” Merriam-Webster.com Dictionary, Merriam-Webster, https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/pagination. Page last updated 1 July 2026 (examples updated).
