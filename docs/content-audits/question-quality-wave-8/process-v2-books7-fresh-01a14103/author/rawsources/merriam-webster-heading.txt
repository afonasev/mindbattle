heading, noun sense 1a: something that forms or serves as a head; especially an inscription, headline, or title standing at the top or beginning (as of a letter or chapter). Sense 1b: address and date at the beginning of a letter showing place and time of origin. Other senses concern direction and a heading category.

First known noun use in sense 1a: 1650. Citation block: “Heading.” Merriam-Webster.com Dictionary, Merriam-Webster, https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/heading.
