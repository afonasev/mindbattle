L172@P19: • If an object is allowed to rotate
L173@P19: about a pivot point, it will come to rest with its center of gravity (CG) vertically below the pivot point. The reason is that when the CG is vertically below the pivot, the weight exerts no torque about the
L174@P19: pivot. Thus, there is no tendency

L179@P20: • The center of gravity of a homogeneous symmetrical object will
L180@P20: be in the geometric center. The center of gravity of an irregularly-shaped object will usually be somewhere within the volume of the object; however, for some objects, the center of gravity may be outside of the object itself. For example, the center of gravity of a good high jumper passes under the bar when the high jumper goes over the bar.
