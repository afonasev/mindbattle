L200@P9-10: 10Correct lubrication can already be difficult for individual points. The challenge is to do it right for all points. A
L201@P10: common description of a reliable lubrication approach
L202@P10: is the “5-R-Method”:
L203@P10: The right lubricant needs to be applied in the right
L204@P10: amount and with the right method at the right time to
L205@P10: the right lubrication point. By adding a sixth “R”, we
L206@P10: highlight the right connectivity method.
L207@P10: How to ensure lubrication reliability?
L208@P10: In simple terms, lubrication reduces friction and wear
L209@P10: of surfaces by avoiding metal-to-metal contact by placing lubricant between the surfaces.
L210@P10: A lubricant can be solid (such as graphite), liquid (oil),
L211@P10: semi-solid (grease) or gas (air). Each has a specific viscosity (resistance to flow) that, combined with the relative speed of the mating surfaces, the load and the
L212@P10: wedge created by the geometry of the components, will
L213@P10: create a thin lubricant film. The lubricant film is typically in the order of microns or sub-microns that the
L214@P10: human eye cannot usually detect.
L215@P10: Oils and greases are the most important lubricants
L216@P10: used today. Additives can be incorporated into them to
L217@P10: add, or remove, certain properties, such as extreme
L218@P10: pressure, anti-wear and anti-oxidant. For greases, a
L219@P10: thickening agent holds the oil together and gives the
L220@P10: lubricant a consistency that enables it to stay in place.

L302@P14: Before choosing the right lubricant for specific operating conditions, more aspects must be considered such
L303@P14: as the operating environment and temperature.
L304@P14: Close to the sea, the risk of corrosion is high and
L305@P14: greases whose additive package and/or thickener are
L306@P14: specially designed to withstand water or chemical substances are a perfect choice.
L307@P14: On the ocean, operators have to avoid any water pollution, and lubricants must be biodegradable, environmentally acceptable, and certified.
L308@P14: The same attention is needed in the food and beverage
L309@P14: industry, where even NSF H1-approved machinery
L310@P14: lubricants are only allowed to get into incidental contact with the food whenever that cannot be avoided.
L311@P14: Morning temperatures can be very low in the mountains
L312@P14: or even in some deserts, so the lubricant should be
L313@P14: highly thermal and mechanically stable when a
L314@P14: machine operates in these environments.

L316@P14: good pumpability properties to avoid piping obstruction, oil separation, and subsequent leakage, blockage,
L317@P14: or stalling.
