## Anatomy of a Book
Boards:
The covers of a hard bound book; the boards are stiff wood, cardboard, or paperboard which is covered with cloth, leather or paper.

Cover:
An outer surface nearly the size of the pages and attached to the spine by a hinge mechanism, i.e. the binding of a book.

Endpapers:
Paper, often of coated stock, marbled, or otherwise decorative papers inside the front and back covers of books. One side of the paper is glued to the cover (this is the pastedown) and the other is “free” next to flyleaves or main text block.

## Other Terms
Dust jacket:
The paper, often with illustrations and information about the book, used as a protective covering over the book; sometimes called a book jacket or a dust wrapper.

Slipcase:
A box with one open side, into which a volume or a multivolme set is “slipped” for protection.
