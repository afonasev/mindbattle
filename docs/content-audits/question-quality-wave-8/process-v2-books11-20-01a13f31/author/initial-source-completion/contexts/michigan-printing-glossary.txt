Locator: lines 286-348, entries Imposition and Scoring.

L297 Dummy: an unprinted mock-up of a book, brochure, or item to be printed.
L313 Imposition: arrangement of pages in a printing form so that, when the printed sheet is folded and trimmed, the pages appear in proper sequence.
L345 Scoring: pressing a channel into a sheet so that it folds more easily.
L348 Self-cover: a booklet whose cover is made from the same paper as the inside/text pages.
