URL: https://printorders.unt.edu/resources/glossary.html
Locator: entries Collate, Gather, Gathering, Imposition, Signature (Section).

Collate: to gather sheets or signatures together in their correct order.
Gather: to assemble or collect sections into single copies of complete books for binding.
Gathering: assembling sheets of paper and signatures into their proper sequence; collating.
Imposition: arrangement of pages so that they print correctly on a press sheet and are in proper order when folded.
Signature (Section): a printed sheet (or its flat) consisting of book pages placed so that they will fold and bind together as a section; the printed sheet after folding.
