L21-24 Book Binding: Perfect Bound means pages and laminated cover are glued together at the spine.
L28-38 Book Cover Options: case laminate cover; case laminate is a laminated cover pasted on cover boards and folded over the edges. Jacketed Case Laminate is a case laminate cover that includes a laminated jacket folded over the cover. Digital Cloth Cover has a subtle cloth-like look. Hardcover is available with or without a dust jacket.
