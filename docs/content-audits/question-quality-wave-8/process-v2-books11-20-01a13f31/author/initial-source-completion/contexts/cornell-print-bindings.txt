L28 Saddle stitch documents need to be built in page multiples of 4 pages (8, 12, 16, etc.) because they are printed on sheets that are folded in half.
L29 Flat size is after printing and necessary trimming but before operations such as folding.
L30 Folded size is the document dimensions after it has been folded.
L31 PDFs should be sent as single pages and not spreads.
L53 Perfect Bind: cover and pages are glued together at the spine and then the edges are trimmed to give sharp, even edges. Perfect bound books are lightweight, flexible, and sleek.
L54 Saddle Stitch: pages are gathered, folded and stapled along the crease from the outside; booklet page count needs to be in multiples of four.
L55 Coil Binding: a continuous plastic loop is inserted into punched holes along the spine; this allows books to lay flat when open.
L56 Tape Binding: a strip of tape lined with heat-activated or pressure-sensitive adhesive is applied to the spine of a stack of paper; it is less durable than perfect binding.
