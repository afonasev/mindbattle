L41 ‘Copy’ is another word for text that is to be published, from a company report to a book to product packaging (or anything else with words).
L43 In the traditional publishing workflow, copyediting comes after developmental and stylistic line editing, and involves:
L45 agreeing final content
L46 checking grammar, spelling and punctuation
L47 applying consistency to the language and formatting
L48 making sure it’s clear and reads well for its intended purpose.
L49 Once a text has been copyedited, it’s ready for design and layout, and then proofreading.
L52 Copyeditors work with readers, authors, publishers and designers/typesetters.
