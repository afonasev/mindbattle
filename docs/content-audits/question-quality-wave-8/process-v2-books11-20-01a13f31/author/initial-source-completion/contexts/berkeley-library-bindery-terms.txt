L62-63 Case: a book cover consisting of two boards and a spine inlay covered with paper, cloth, leather or another material. It is made separately from the text block and later attached at casing-in; a cased-in book is often called a hardcover.
L64-65 Casing in: applying adhesive to the outside endpapers of a text block and fitting the text block into its case.
L73-74 Collation: checking books/periodicals for completeness and suitability for binding; also preparing and arranging material in sequence for binding.
L125-126 Hardcover: a flexible cover material, usually cloth or paper, supported by rigid boards.
L206-207 Perfect bind: a high-speed binding process used in trade binderies to bind books.
L234 Refold: one printed sheet of a book folded to fit within the text block.
L292-293 Text block: a gathering of printed or written leaves, excluding added endpapers and other binder material.
L297 Turn-in: covering material that extends beyond the boards before folding over them; the turn-in protects the boards and inlay from wear.
L Signature (section or gathering): one printed sheet folded one, two, three or four (rarely five) times to make a section, commonly 8, 16 or 32 pages.
