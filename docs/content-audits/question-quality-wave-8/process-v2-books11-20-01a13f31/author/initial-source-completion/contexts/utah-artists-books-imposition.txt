Locator: lines 10-13, definition of imposition.

L13 Imposition is the process of planning the order of a book's pages for efficient and accurate printing and binding. Multiple images or text units are printed on a printer's sheet in a single run, often with a second run to print the sheet's reverse. The printer's sheet is then cut into spreads if needed and folded into signatures or as appropriate to book structure.
