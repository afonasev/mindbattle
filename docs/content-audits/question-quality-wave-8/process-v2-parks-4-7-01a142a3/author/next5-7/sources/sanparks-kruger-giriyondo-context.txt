Giriyondo Access Point

Kruger National Park, with the Parque Nacional do Limpopo, and Gonarezhou National Park form part of the Great Limpopo Transfrontier Park, a cross-border conservation initiative between Zimbabwe, South Africa and Mozambique.

With the Giriyondo Access Facility between South Africa and Mozambique operational, there are new travel routes open to park visitors.
