FAQ question: What is Royalty?
Answer: Royalty refers to the payment an author receives from the sale of their books. The primary author(s) who write the book are eligible for Royalties.

The agency's separate “Royalty Structure and Copyright” page states a 15% print-price rate in its described contract arrangement and explains alternatives when copyright is retained or transferred. The present card uses only the FAQ's general author-payment definition, not a universal rate.
