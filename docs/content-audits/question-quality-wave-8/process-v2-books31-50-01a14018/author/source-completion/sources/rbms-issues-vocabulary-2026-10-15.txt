Comment field: Determination of “issue” is considerably more complicated than determination of “state” and these terms must be applied with care. Though exact definitions vary, “issue” refers to a consciously planned publishing unit whereas “state” applies to differences (often resulting from an error or attempt to correct the error) among printed sheets of a single impression or issue.

The vocabulary lists binding issues, part-issued books in volume form, re-issues, and simultaneous issues as narrower terms.
