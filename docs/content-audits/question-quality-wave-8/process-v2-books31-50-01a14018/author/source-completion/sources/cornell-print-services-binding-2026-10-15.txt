“Perfect Bind: Perfect binding refers to the method of binding where the cover and pages of the book are glued together at the spine and then the edges are ‘perfectly’ trimmed to give your book its sharp, even edges.”

“Flat Size” is the dimensions after printing and any needed trimming, before operations such as folding. Bleed extends beyond the page edge to allow room to trim.
