P11 L273–275: “The ISSN was devised as a standard code for the unique identification of a specific continuing resource, i.e. a specific diachronic work with indeterminate extension plan either successive (e.g. a serial) or integrating (e.g. a database), in a defined medium.”

P17 L468–473: One ISSN is assigned to a continuing resource in a defined medium; it is permanently linked to its key title, a standardized form of title derived from the resource. A key title must be unique to that resource in the ISSN Portal.

P25 L699–702: ISSN assignment to a specific title does not grant exclusive rights to use it; a unique key title may add qualifying details when the publication title is not distinctive.

P60 L1794–1799: ISSN assignment carries no meaning or legal evidence of rights ownership and does not imply endorsement or quality.

P191 L5294–5296: ISSN is the eight-digit number defined by ISO 3297 (including check digit), preceded by prefix ISSN and assigned to a continuing resource by the ISSN Network.

P191 L5322–5327: A journal is a periodical for a subject/profession; journals can be print or digital.

These passages support the continuing-resource / serial-title category, unique-title relation, and eight-digit structure.
