DCRM (RDA Edition) — 3.22 Designation of edition

Retrieved 2026-10-15; complete section 3.22 (L152–254) opened and personally read.

3.22.2.1 RDA definition and scope: A word, character, or group of words or characters that identifies an edition to which a manifestation belongs.

3.22.31.1 Designations of edition normally include the word “edition” (or its equivalent) or a related term such as “revision” or “issue.”

3.22.32.1 Use judgment for “impression” or “printing” as an edition designation. For publications from the hand-press era, those statements usually signal a new edition or issue.

3.22.32.2 For machine-press resources or resources manufactured by engraving, “impression” or “printing” is more likely to indicate a new impression of the same edition.

3.22.33.1 Omit statements relating to a substantially unchanged impression or state from transcription; record them as an item note if important.
