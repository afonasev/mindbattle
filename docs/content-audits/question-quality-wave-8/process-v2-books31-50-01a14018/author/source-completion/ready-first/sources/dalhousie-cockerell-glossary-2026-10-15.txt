Retrieved 2026-10-15; complete relevant glossary body opened and read.

Fore edge: The edge of the book opposite the spine, the side on which it is opened.
Head and Tail: The top and bottom of the book.
Headband: a strip of leather or cord sewn into or glued at the head and sometimes the tail of a book for protection and reinforcement; in elaborate bindings coloured silk or linen threads are wound around the cord in sequence to create a decorative pattern.
Signature: A section of a book formed from a folded sheet.
Spine: The back of the book, where the sections are sewn or attached and which is covered by the spine of the binding.
