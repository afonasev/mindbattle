Endpapers: Papers or leaves placed at the end and beginning of the book to protect the text and act as part of the attachment between the book and the cover boards.

Mull: An open-weave variety of coarse, sized muslin used for reinforcing and, to some extent, stiffening the backbone and joints of the book. Sometimes called super.

Section: A single binding unit of leaves, folded together to be fastened with other sections to form the text block. Bibliographers often use the term gathering, and printers use the term signature.

Spine: The portion of the cover which fits over the backbone. Super: See Mull. Tail: The bottom of the page, or the bottom edge.
