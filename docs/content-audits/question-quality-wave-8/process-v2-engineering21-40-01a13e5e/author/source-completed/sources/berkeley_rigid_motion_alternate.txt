L7: # Kinematics of rigid bodies
L8: 
L9: Here, we discuss how rotations feature in the kinematics of rigid bodies. Specifically, we present various representations of a rigid-body motion, establish expressions for the relative velocity and acceleration of two points on a body, and compare several axes and angles of rotation associated with the motion of a rigid body.
