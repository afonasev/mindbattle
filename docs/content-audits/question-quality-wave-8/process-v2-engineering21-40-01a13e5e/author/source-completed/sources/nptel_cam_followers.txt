L25: 2.1.1 Knife edge follower
L26: 
L27: The contacting end of the follower has a sharp knife edge. A sliding motion exists between the contacting cam and follower surfaces. It is rarely used in practice because the small area of contacting surface results in excessive wear.
L28: 
L29: 2.1.2  Roller follower
L30: It consists of a cylindrical roller which rolls on cam surface. Because of the rolling motion between the contacting surfaces, the rate of wear is reduced in comparison with Knife edge follower. The roller followers are extensively used where more space is available such as gas and oil engines.
L31: 
L32: 2.1.3  Flat face follower
L33: 
L34: The follower face is perfectly flat. It experiences a side thrust due to the friction between contact surfaces of follower and cam.
L35: 
L36: 2.1.4 Spherical face follower
L37: The contacting end of the follower is of spherical shape which overcomes the drawback of side thrust as experiences by flat face follower.
