Head and Tail: the top and the bottom of the book respectively.
Headband: a strip of leather or cord sewn into or glued at the head and sometimes the tail of a book for protection and reinforcement; elaborate bindings may wrap coloured silk or linen threads around the cord decoratively.
Page lines 30-31 state definitions primarily based on Douglas Cockerell, Bookbinding, and the Care of Books, 4th ed. (1924), with additional dictionaries/glossaries consulted.
