Book Cloth: a sized and coated textile specially prepared for binding books.
Buckram: a strong book-cloth woven from cotton or linen, now often coated with acrylic.
Endpapers: papers or leaves placed at the beginning and end of a book to protect the text and act as part of the attachment between book and cover boards; commonly an outer pastedown and at least one fly leaf.
Super: “See Mull.” (Cornell Glossary lines 474-477.)
