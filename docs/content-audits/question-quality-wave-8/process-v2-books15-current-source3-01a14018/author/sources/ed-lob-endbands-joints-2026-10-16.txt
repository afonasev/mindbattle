Endbands URL: https://lob.is.ed.ac.uk/concept/2370
Locator: lines 10-15.
Definition: components at the head and tail of the spine; headbands and tailbands can be sewn with thread/thongs or attached with adhesive. They may be decorative; structurally important endbands can reinforce the bookblock and board attachment. The generic use of endband includes structural and decorative bands; headband/tailband specify position.
Joints (places) URL: https://lob.is.ed.ac.uk/concept/1405
Locator: lines 0-10, with source reference at lines 12-20.
Definition: area along the spine edges along which boards and/or cover hinge; outer and inner joints are distinguished. Source reference names Roberts, Etherington and Brown, Bookbinding and the Conservation of Books: A Dictionary of Descriptive Terminology (Library of Congress, 1982).
