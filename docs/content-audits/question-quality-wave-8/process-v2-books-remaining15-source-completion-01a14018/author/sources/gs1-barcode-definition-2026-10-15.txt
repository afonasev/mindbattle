L1 What is a barcode?
L3 Modified on Fri, 30 Aug, 2024 at 1:56 PM.
L5 A barcode is a machine-readable representation of information using patterns of different widths of bars and spaces.
L7 Barcodes can be read by optical scanners called bar code readers or scanned from an image by special software. Barcodes are widely used to implement Automatic Identification & Data Capture (AIDC) systems that improve the speed and accuracy of computer data entry.
L8 Barcodes with parallel bars and spaces are one-dimensional symbols; patterns of small squares are two-dimensional. GS1-endorsed barcodes allow GS1 Identification Keys to be used on trade items, locations, logistic units, and assets.
L9 More complex barcodes such as GS1-128, GS1 DataBar, GS1 QR Code, GS1 DataMatrix, and GS1 DotCode allow attributes such as batch numbers and expiry dates to be encoded using GS1 Application Identifiers.
