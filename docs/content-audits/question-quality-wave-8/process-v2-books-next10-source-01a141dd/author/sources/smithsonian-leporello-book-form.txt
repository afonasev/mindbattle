“Folding mechanisms: Some books are designed to open like an accordion, or to fan out and form a circle. Books that unfold can take a variety of forms, which are called leporellos, carousels, and tunnel books or peep-shows.”

“Leporello: An accordion book formed of one long sheet of folded paper that stretches out in a zigzag or concertina shape.”
