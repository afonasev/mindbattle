“codex (pl. codices)
Brown (1994) defines codex as ‘...a book composed of folded sheets sewn along one edge, distinct from other writing vehicles such as the roll or tablet...’.”

“collation
Description of a book's physical structure. That is, the arrangement and construction of quires and booklets within the codex.”

“quire
A group of folios folded and stitched together. Quires are the units of which the codex is constructed.”
